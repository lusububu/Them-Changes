Take it with you
  1. Pick a keyin Settings, or let Find the key work it out from notes you hear.
  2. Load a progressionfrom the menu in the Chord Builder, or build your own: click roots on the circle, try colours, and the app explains every move.
  3. Shape the soundwith the Wurlitzer 200A in the Chord Builder and the 808 in the Beat Builder, then press Play in the bar at the bottom.

Open a progression and press Play in the bar at the bottom. Tap any chord to hear it alone. Not sure of the song's key? Use Find the key in Settings.

Pick a root under Key in Settings and a chord type to see its notes and a voicing.

Notes and typeface
Notes

Voicings are one good option each, not the only way to play a chord. Chord Builder feedback compares your moves with common Soul, R&B and gospel patterns; it is a guide, and plenty of great records break these rules on purpose. Note names use simplified sharp/flat spellings.
